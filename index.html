Safety

Exactly what it changes.
Nothing hidden.

Most "boosters" are bloatware. Here's the full list, so you know.

What it does

  • Windows settings of your account (HKCU registry): Game Mode, Game Bar recording, mouse acceleration, notifications, background apps, ads, animations
  • Power plan set to High performance with powercfg, Windows' own tool
  • Game Boost closes apps from a fixed list and can relaunch them
  • Your games get high CPU priority and your fastest GPU while they run
  • A backup before every change: "Undo all" puts everything back

What it never does

  • No Windows services disabled, no system-wide (HKLM) changes
  • No NVIDIA / AMD driver settings touched
  • Nothing injected into your games, no game files changed
  • No account, no tracking: nothing is sent anywhere, all stays in %APPDATA%\PhantomBoost
  • No admin needed, except the optional FPS counter

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no premium version, no ads, no account.

Can I get banned?

No. Phantom Boost never touches your game: no injection, no memory reading, no game files changed. The crosshair and HUDs are normal transparent windows on top of the screen, and the color tuning is done by Windows itself.

Is there a virus in it?

No. You can scan the installer yourself on VirusTotal. Some antivirus apps are wary of any new unsigned app; the "Safety" section above lists exactly what it changes.

Will I really get more FPS?

It depends on your PC. Game Boost and the optimizer free up CPU, RAM and bandwidth and remove stutters, which helps most on mid-range PCs and laptops. On a high-end PC, the big wins are stretched res, colors and the crosshair.

How do I undo everything?

Optimizer → "Undo all" restores your exact previous Windows values. Game Boost → "Bring them back" relaunches the apps it closed. Closing the app puts your screen colors and resolution back.

How do updates work?

Phantom Boost checks GitHub, downloads new versions in the background and installs them the next time it starts. Your settings, crosshairs and HUDs stay as they are.
