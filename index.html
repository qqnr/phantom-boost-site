FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Phantom Boost is free, with no trial, no account and no paid version.

Does it touch my game files?

No. Phantom Boost doesn't modify or inject anything into your games. Colors use Windows display settings, the crosshair and HUDs are separate windows on top of the game, and stretch uses the normal Windows resolution change. Check the rules of the games you play if you're unsure about overlays.

How do updates work?

Phantom Boost checks for a new version when it starts and every few hours. It downloads it in the background, checks the file, and installs it the next time it starts, in a few seconds. Your settings stay as they are. You can turn this off in Settings.

Which Windows versions work?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.

My screen didn't go back to normal. What do I do?

Turn tuning off in the app, or press its hotkey. Closing Phantom Boost also puts your colors and resolution back. Stretch is undone by itself even if the app crashes.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Phantom Boost → Uninstall. Your screen goes back to normal and "Start with Windows" is removed.
